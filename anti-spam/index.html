Anti-Spam Compliance

Our approach to permission-based email, SMS and other electronic communications.

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. Our communication standards

Elevated Window Cleanings, LLC aims to send relevant, lawful messages, avoid misleading sender details or subject lines, and respect recipient preferences. Transactional messages related to a requested quote or active job are distinct from promotional campaigns, but each communication must still comply with applicable law.

2. How contacts are collected

Contacts may come from estimate requests, service inquiries, bookings, customer relationships or another lawful source. We do not treat mere website visits or an unselected marketing checkbox as SMS marketing consent. Where a particular communication requires prior consent, the applicable consent must be captured and documented.

3. Marketing email rules

Commercial marketing emails should accurately identify the sender, use nondeceptive subject lines, include a working unsubscribe mechanism, disclose promotional status where required and include Elevated's valid postal contact address in the email itself. We will honor marketing email opt-out requests within the legally required time frame. A postal address has not been supplied for inclusion on this website; it must be configured in the email sending system before commercial campaigns launch.

4. Text messaging rules

SMS programs must use valid consent for their message category, identify the sender, provide appropriate message-rate/frequency disclosures and support opt-out. Reply STOP to opt out of texts, or contact us directly. See SMS & Opt-In Policy.

5. Reporting unwanted messages

If you believe you received an unwanted or erroneous message claiming to be from Elevated, contact ElevatedCleanings1@gmail.com or (208) 982-0073 with the date and nature of the message. Avoid sending sensitive personal details in your report. We will review and address requests as appropriate.

6. Vendors and updates

We may use providers such as GoHighLevel/Waypoint Systems to manage communications. We remain responsible for configuring our communications and handling subscriber requests appropriately. We may update this policy as practices evolve.
