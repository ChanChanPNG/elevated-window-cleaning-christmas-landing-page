Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information when you visit our site or request services.

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. Who we are

This policy describes the privacy practices of Elevated Window Cleanings, LLC (referred to as “Elevated,” “we,” or “us”) for this website and inquiries submitted through it. Questions: ElevatedCleanings1@gmail.com or (208) 982-0073.

2. Information you provide

When you request a quote, schedule a service, contact us or become a customer, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, property or project details, preferred appointment times, messages and other information you choose to provide.

3. Information collected automatically

When enabled, cookies, pixels and related technologies can collect IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, referral information, and interactions with advertisements or the website. Meta Pixel may be used for advertising measurement or remarketing when deployed; its presence on this particular landing page has not been confirmed. See your browser and applicable consent controls for available choices.

4. How information is used

  • Respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, schedule services and communicate about jobs.
  • Deliver services, process payments, maintain records and provide customer support.
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders, estimates, follow-ups and review requests, where permitted.
  • Send marketing communications where lawfully authorized; you can opt out.
  • Protect our systems, measure site performance and improve services.

5. Service providers and third-party tools

Depending on the workflow, we use or may use GoHighLevel (through Waypoint Systems) for forms, CRM and communications; Jobber for service management; Stripe for payment processing; and Meta technologies for advertising measurement. These providers may process information on our behalf or under their own terms. This page does not mean every integration is installed on every Elevated site. Payment card details entered in third-party checkout are handled by the payment processor rather than published on this website.

6. SMS information and sharing

Mobile information and consent collected for SMS will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Sharing with messaging service providers solely to deliver communications on our behalf is permitted where necessary. See our SMS & Opt-In Policy.

7. Retention, security and rights

We retain data as reasonably needed for service delivery, business records, legal compliance and dispute resolution, and use reasonable safeguards. No transmission or storage method is guaranteed secure. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or information about use of personal data. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We may verify your request and retain information where required or permitted by law.

8. External sites and minors

Our site may link to third-party websites governed by their own privacy practices. This site is intended for adults arranging property services, not directed to children under 13. Do not submit sensitive information through the public estimate form.

9. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy and post a new effective date. Questions or requests: ElevatedCleanings1@gmail.com or (208) 982-0073.
