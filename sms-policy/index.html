SMS & Opt-In Policy

How Elevated handles text-message consent, service updates and marketing communications.

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. Program and message types

Elevated Window Cleanings, LLC may send SMS/text messages in connection with requested estimates, appointment confirmations, reminders, scheduling updates, service follow-ups, review requests and, if separately authorized as required, promotional offers or marketing updates.

2. How you opt in

Consent may be obtained through a clearly labeled checkbox or other documented opt-in process at the point where a phone number is collected, or through another legally valid consent method. The consent language must identify Elevated Window Cleanings as the sender and describe the applicable message types. Marketing consent must not be prechecked or made a condition of buying services. Submitting an estimate form alone does not constitute consent to promotional texts.

3. Message disclosures

Message frequency varies based on your service activity and preferences. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to an Elevated text to opt out, or HELP for assistance where supported by the messaging program. You may also contact ElevatedCleanings1@gmail.com or (208) 982-0073 to request that messages stop. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

4. Opt-out and help

We will honor valid opt-out and consent-revocation requests as required by law and will not continue the affected messages after processing an opt-out, except for a permissible confirmation or communications otherwise authorized by law. If you change your mind, re-enrollment requires a valid new opt-in.

5. Privacy and mobile data

We use contact and messaging details to deliver requested communications and operate the messaging program. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing. We may share necessary information with messaging platforms and service providers acting on our behalf to send or manage messages. Read our Privacy Policy.

6. Important implementation note

The live form may be managed by GoHighLevel/Waypoint Systems. Any opt-in choices and consent records must be collected and retained in the actual form or communication workflow; this policy alone does not create consent. Website visitors should review the checkbox or disclosure presented when submitting information.

7. Contact

Questions about messaging or support: ElevatedCleanings1@gmail.com or (208) 982-0073.
